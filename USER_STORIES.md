# User stories

| ID | As an athlete, I want to… | Requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| US-01 | Download a template and import my externally authored training or event goals and dated workouts. | R-70, R-73 |
| US-02 | Export and reimport the plan so that the file carries the complete schedule and completion history. | R-71 |
| US-03 | Understand what to fix after a bad import and keep my active plan while I fix it. | R-72 |
| US-04 | See my next seven days and open a separate history section to review past planned workouts and synced Garmin activities from the plan period. | R-73 |
| US-05 | Edit or move an incomplete upcoming workout, swap it with another workout in the schedule, or move an unstarted missed workout forward. | R-75 |
| US-06 | Mark a workout complete, including when I only did part of it, and optionally record what I did and my perceived exertion. | R-74 |
| US-07 | Preview and approve week-bounded Chat changes informed by my plan and most recent Garmin activity, with a fresh proposal after intervening plan changes. | R-76, R-77 |
| US-08 | Sync from Plan or Settings and export activity summaries in CSV and Markdown. | R-28, R-78, R-79 |
